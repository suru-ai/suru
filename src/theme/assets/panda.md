# Panda

`panda.json` adapts the [Panda Theme](https://github.com/PandaTheme) palette
and syntax roles from its [Atom theme](https://github.com/PandaTheme/atom-panda-syntax/tree/master/styles)
and [Hyper terminal theme](https://github.com/PandaTheme/hyperterm-panda/blob/master/index.js).

Panda keeps its original dark palette in both appearance modes. The panel and
selection grays follow Atom's lightened background; diff backgrounds are tinted
for Suru. UI roles use the upstream blue, pink, mint, and orange accents.
