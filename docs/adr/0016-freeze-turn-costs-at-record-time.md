# Freeze a Turn's Cost when its Usage is recorded

Every Turn's Cost is computed once, when its Usage is recorded, and stored with the Turn; nothing ever restates a stored Cost against later prices. A figure the Provider states itself (Claude's `total_cost_usd`, Copilot pricing from its own `billing.tokenPrices`) outranks one Suru estimates from a rate table (models.dev, fetched and cached, refreshed at most daily), and each Cost carries a Cost Basis — Reported or Estimated — saying who computed the number rather than whether money changed hands, since a subscription plan can make even a Reported figure cost nothing marginal. Where no price is known the Cost is absent, never zero, so free and unknown never blur. The alternative — recomputing from current prices at display time — was rejected because it silently rewrites history whenever the catalog moves, and it makes a Turn's Cost vanish retroactively when a model leaves the catalog; opencode reached the same conclusion (cost persisted per message at step end), and its one clear mistake — rendering unknown pricing as $0 — is what the absent-not-zero rule exists to avoid.

## Consequences

A stored Cost survives catalog changes, model retirements, and pricing-source outages, but it also inherits whatever the rate table said on the day it was recorded — a wrong price fixed later leaves old Turns priced by the wrong one, which is accepted: Estimated Costs are estimates. Cost Basis is an enum rather than a bool so a billed-versus-included dimension can be added later without touching stored data, once per-provider auth-mode detection exists to support it.
