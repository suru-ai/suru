# Hold a Turn open while its Compaction outlasts the Provider's loop

Copilot compacts a Session's context in the background of the Session rather than inside a run of its agent loop, so the idle that ADR 0015 settles a Copilot Turn on can arrive while a compaction begun in that Turn is still summarising. We decided that the idle is held: the Turn stays open, and the Session Working, until the Compaction settles, and the Turn then settles as the idle said. Settling at the idle would have settled the still-running Compaction as failed, the rule for work a Turn leaves open (ADR 0039), and Copilot's completion would then have recorded a second Compaction in a Continuation — one occasion recorded as two, the first of them false. While the Turn is held it is a working Turn like any other. A Prompt steered into it runs Copilot's stopped loop again, and that loop's output and its next idle belong to the held Turn, whichever comes first of the loop's output and the compaction's end. A queued Prompt waits for the Turn to settle and begins the next. An interrupt cancels the compaction (`session.history.cancelBackgroundCompaction`, which the loop's abort does not reach) and settles the Turn and its Compaction as interrupted itself, since a stopped loop has nothing left to report — unless Subagents still work past it, when the abort they need ends in an idle of the loop's own, and that idle settles the Turn. The hold lasts until Copilot answers the cancel, and the next Turn is not begun before the interrupt resolves, so nothing it still has to stop reaches that Turn. A cancel Copilot fails stops nothing, and the compaction is followed as before; a failure Copilot reported while the cancel was out stands as the compaction's own whenever the cancel failed or found nothing to cancel. A cancel that finds nothing to cancel came too late: the compaction had already ended, and its outcome stands as Copilot reports it — completed with its counts and summary, or failed with its error — whichever of that report and the answer to the cancel reaches Suru first. The interrupt waits for the report to reach the timeline, under the bound every request it makes waits under, so that it lands in the Turn the compaction held, ahead of the idle of any abort the interrupt still sends and of anything of the next Turn; a Turn only the compaction held was then stopped by nothing, and settles as its loop's idle said. Only a report that never arrives within the bound leaves the Turn and its Compaction to settle as interrupted. A compaction Copilot begins while no Turn runs, or begins in a Continuation that late output opened, makes that Continuation Copilot's own (`ContinuationStarted`): the compaction settling settles it, and the next Prompt interrupts it first, cancelling the compaction, as it does a native Continuation of Claude's.

## Considered Options

Settling at the idle, as ADR 0015 reads literally, was rejected for the misrecorded Compaction above. Recording the Compaction only once it ended was rejected because its row would never be Active, and a reader could not see that the Provider was still working on the Session. ADR 0015 rejected holding a Turn open until its Subagents settle; this hold is different in kind. It waits on one summarising call over the Session's own context rather than on delegated work of unbounded length, a Prompt is steered or queued as for any working Turn rather than bending the one-active-Turn rule, and an interrupt ends it at once.

## Consequences

A Copilot Turn can read Working after Copilot's loop has gone idle. An interrupt that reaches Copilot only once the compaction holding a Turn has ended stops nothing, so that Turn reads as having worked rather than stopped, and the interrupt takes as long to resolve as Copilot's report of that end takes to arrive, up to the bound. A steer during the hold reaches a loop that Copilot runs as a fresh stretch, and Suru records it in the held Turn. A Subagent's compaction is not held: one outlasting the Subagent's stretch settles with that stretch, and Copilot's later report of its end records nothing — in particular not in the stretch that resumes the Subagent. One Copilot begins while the Subagent works no stretch wakes it into a Continuation of its own Session, which the compaction's end settles.
